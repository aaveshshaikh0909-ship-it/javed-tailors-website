THE STORY

Built around tailoring skill and a personal fit.

Javed Tailors is built around Javed Bhai's long experience in men's tailoring. The goal is simple: understand what the customer wants, take the right measurements, and make the garment carefully.

The Javed Tailors brand began in 2021 and is now being developed online so customers and businesses can discover the tailoring service more easily.

MEASUREMENT & REFERRAL

Simple benefits for your next order.

We want the ordering process to be convenient, especially for larger custom orders and customers who refer friends or family.

01

At-home measurement

Orders of ₹3,500 or more: we can come to your location for measurement, subject to area and appointment availability.

For orders below ₹3,500, an at-home measurement visit is chargeable.

02

Refer & save

Refer 3 people who come to Javed Tailors and complete a stitching order.

Once all 3 referrals complete their orders, the referring customer gets 30% off their next stitching order.
