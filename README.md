## Current customer policies shown on the site
- Orders of ₹3,500 or more: at-home measurement can be arranged, subject to area and appointment availability.
- Orders below ₹3,500: at-home measurement visit is chargeable.
- Referral offer: refer 3 people who complete a stitching order; the referring customer receives 30% off their next stitching order.

## Visual policy
- The Styles & Inspiration gallery uses reference/inspiration visuals and does not present them as Javed Tailors customer work.
- The previous Javed-labelled fabric photo has been removed from the live website design for now.

## Deployment
The project is a static HTML/CSS/JS site and can be deployed to Netlify. Connecting the project to GitHub enables continuous deployment for future updates.
